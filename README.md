## 🌍 The Problem
By 2035, the world needs millions of electric vehicles to meet climate goals, but the transition is entirely unequal and supply chains are fragmented. 
* **Canada** is pushing toward a 75% EV sales goal by 2035, driven by four-wheelers and deep investments in domestic battery manufacturing.
* **Pakistan** is targeting 30% EV adoption by 2030, driven primarily by an exploding micro-mobility market (2-wheelers and 3-wheelers). 

Investors, policymakers, and manufacturers lack a unified, accessible tool to compare these divergent market trajectories and visualize where critical materials (like Lithium, Cobalt, and Nickel) are flowing.

## 💡 The Solution
**EVSync** is an interactive, open-source intelligence dashboard built with Python and Streamlit. It bridges the data gap by providing:
1. **Interactive Supply Chain Mapping:** Visualizing the flow of raw materials from mining to vehicle assembly.
2. **Predictive Market Comparisons:** Using Scikit-Learn polynomial regression to forecast when different nations will actually hit their legislative EV targets.
3. **Hyper-Local Context:** Contrasting mature markets (Canada) with emerging micro-mobility markets (Pakistan).

---

## 🚀 Features

* **Global Overview Dashboard:** High-level metrics on market value, carbon reduction, and vehicle adoption.
* **Scikit-Learn Forecasting:** Real-time predictive modeling trained on historical adoption data to project future market saturation.
* **Interactive Data Visualizations:** Built with Plotly to allow users to scrub through timelines (2026–2040) and filter by vehicle types.
* **Policy Tracker:** Integrated insights on the latest governmental mandates and infrastructure subsidies.
